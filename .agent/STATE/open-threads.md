# Open Threads

> In-flight work and unresolved questions. Updated by `close-session.sh`
> from the SESSION CLOSE `GAPS_SURFACED` and `OPEN_QUESTIONS` fields.
> Items are removed when resolved (closed in a later session).

---

## Format

Each entry:

```
### THREAD-NNN: <title>
**Opened:** YYYY-MM-DD  |  **Session:** <slug>
**Status:** open | blocked | in-progress
**Description:** <what is unresolved and why it matters>
**Next action:** <what needs to happen to close this>
```
